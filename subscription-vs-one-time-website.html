Website Subscription vs One-Time Build

Two ways to pay for a custom website: a low monthly subscription with no money down, or a traditional one-time build fee. Here is a clear, side-by-side comparison of both models — plus why Apex now builds every site on a simple monthly subscription, with hosting, maintenance, and updates always included.

When the subscription wins

If you want to launch without a big cheque, keep cash in the business, and never think about hosting, security, or updates again, the subscription is the simplest path. It bundles everything into one predictable monthly fee, billed month-to-month with no contract and no minimum term — cancel anytime. This is why Apex builds every site on a monthly subscription, and it is the right choice for most new and small businesses.

When a one-time build makes sense

In the wider industry, a one-time build can suit an established business with budget set aside that wants the lowest possible cost spread over many years — you pay a large fee upfront and then arrange hosting and maintenance separately. Apex has chosen not to work this way: every Apex site is subscription-based, so you are never hit with a big upfront invoice and your hosting, security, and updates are always included.

QuestionsSubscription vs One-Time — FAQ

Is a website subscription cheaper than paying upfront?

It depends on your timeline. A subscription has no large upfront cost — with Apex you start from $149/month with nothing down — so it is cheaper to launch. A traditional one-time build costs more upfront but can cost less over many years. Because most small businesses want to preserve cash flow, Apex builds every site on the monthly subscription, with no upfront build fee at all.

Do I own my website on the subscription plan?

Yes. Your Apex plan bundles your site, hosting, security, and maintenance into one monthly fee, billed month-to-month with no contract and no minimum term. You own your domain and your content, and you can cancel anytime; we can walk you through the specifics for your situation.

Is there a contract or minimum term?

No. Every Apex plan is billed month-to-month with no contract, no minimum term, and no cancellation fee. Your price always includes hosting, security, backups, and updates — there is no forced re-build, and the site keeps running and being improved for as long as you subscribe.

Which option is better for a brand-new business?

For a brand-new business, the subscription is almost always the better fit — it removes the upfront cost barrier, includes everything you need to launch, and lets the site grow as you do. While some established businesses still choose a traditional one-time build elsewhere, Apex builds every site on the monthly subscription so you are never blocked by a big upfront invoice.
