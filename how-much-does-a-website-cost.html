How Much Does a Website Cost?

A professional small-business website typically costs $1,000 to $10,000 as a one-time build, or roughly $150 to $600 per month on an all-inclusive subscription. What you pay depends on how it is built, by whom, and how many pages and features you need. Here is the full breakdown.

What drives the price

The biggest factors are how the site is built (a template on a builder is cheapest; custom hand-coded is the most), the number of pages, and whether you need e-commerce, booking, or custom features. Content and photography, SEO setup, and ongoing maintenance also move the total up or down.

Pay monthly or all at once?

A one-time build costs more upfront but less over several years. A subscription spreads the cost into a flat monthly fee with nothing down, and bundles hosting and maintenance. Which is cheaper depends on your timeline, covered in detail in our subscription vs one-time guide.

QuestionsWebsite Cost — FAQ

How much does a small business website cost?

A professional small-business website typically costs between $1,000 and $10,000 as a one-time build, depending on the number of pages and features. Website builders start near $15 to $50 per month if you build it yourself, and subscription studios like Apex start at $149 per month with nothing upfront, bundling design, hosting, and maintenance.

Is it cheaper to build my own website?

Upfront, yes. A DIY builder like Wix or Squarespace costs $15 to $50 per month with no designer fee. But it costs your time, ships slower and more templated pages, and often needs paid apps to do what you want. For a business that relies on its site to win customers, a professionally built site usually returns more than it costs.

Why do web design prices vary so much?

Price depends on how the site is built and by whom. A template on a builder is cheapest; a freelancer is mid-range; a custom, hand-coded agency build is the most, because every page is engineered for speed, SEO, and your brand. Number of pages, e-commerce, custom features, and ongoing maintenance all move the price up or down.

How much does a website cost per month?

On a subscription model, an all-inclusive small-business website runs roughly $100 to $600 per month, covering design, hosting, security, backups, and maintenance with no large upfront cost. If you paid a one-time build fee instead, expect a smaller ongoing cost of around $20 to $150 per month for hosting and maintenance.

Are there ongoing costs after the website is built?

Yes. Every website has running costs: hosting, a domain, security and SSL, backups, and content updates. With a builder you pay the platform monthly; with a one-time agency build you pay separately for hosting and maintenance; with a subscription studio like Apex, those are bundled into one predictable monthly fee.
