Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Apex Web Studio ("we", "us", "our") designs, builds, and hosts websites and provides a client dashboard that helps small businesses manage the enquiries and customers their websites generate. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website at apexwebstudio.ca and to the dashboard services we provide to our clients.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — such as your name, email address, phone number, business details, and the content of messages you send us when you request a quote, book a call, or contact us.
  • Website usage data — pages visited, referring source, approximate session length, and similar analytics collected to understand how our site performs. We do not use this to build advertising profiles.
  • Client dashboard data — when we provide a dashboard to a client, it stores that client's own business records: their leads, bookings, appointments, and customer messages. This data belongs to our client and is processed on their behalf.
  • Connected messaging accounts — where a client chooses to connect a mailbox or a WhatsApp Business number to their dashboard, we store the access credentials for that account in encrypted form and the messages exchanged, solely to display them in, and send replies from, that client's dashboard.

How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the web design, hosting, and dashboard services you have requested.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website and the dashboard.
  • To send service-related communications (for example, a reply to your enquiry, or a project update).
  • To meet legal, accounting, and security obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to deliver our services. Data is shared with them only as needed to operate the service:

  • Hosting & infrastructure — our website and dashboard run on cloud hosting and database providers that store data on our behalf.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) — where a client connects a WhatsApp Business number, messages are delivered through Meta's WhatsApp Business Cloud API and are subject to Meta's own terms and privacy practices.
  • Email providers — where a client connects a mailbox, messages are retrieved from and sent through that mailbox's email provider.

Connected accounts are always under your control. When you connect a WhatsApp number or mailbox, you grant access only to send and receive messages for the account you chose, you can disconnect it from your dashboard at any time, and disconnecting removes its stored credentials and messages from our system.

Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal obligations. Client dashboard data is retained for the duration of the client relationship and deleted or returned on request when that relationship ends. Credentials and messages for a connected account are deleted when the account is disconnected.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect personal information, including encryption of sensitive credentials at rest, restricted access, and secure transport (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to limit access to those who need it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are an end customer of one of our clients, requests about that client's records are best directed to the client, who controls that data; we will assist them in fulfilling your request.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be communicated where appropriate.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your personal information? Contact Apex Web Studio at contact@apexwebstudio.ca or through the contact form on apexwebstudio.ca.
