QuestionsClient CRM & Automation — FAQ

The questions local business owners ask most before switching on their CRM.

What is the Apex CRM and how does it work?

It's a done-for-you customer system that plugs into your Apex website. It captures every lead, texts back the calls you miss, sends automated Google review requests, books appointments with reminders, and — on the AI plan — even answers the phone. Everything runs under your brand and lands in one simple dashboard.

How much does the CRM cost?

There are two plans: Growth at $399/month and CRM + AI Receptionist at $679/month, plus a one-time $299 setup. Both are month-to-month with no contract — cancel anytime. Multi-location and franchise pricing is custom.

What is missed-call text-back?

The moment you can't answer a call, the system automatically texts the caller a friendly message so the conversation keeps going instead of dying at voicemail. It's the single fastest way to stop losing bookings to the competitor who picks up next.

Do I need a website from Apex to use the CRM?

The CRM is designed to run behind an Apex-built website, where lead forms, calls, and booking all wire together cleanly. We can also connect it to most existing sites — just ask and we'll confirm the fit.

What does the AI receptionist do?

On the AI plan, an AI voice receptionist answers calls 24/7 and books appointments, AI chat handles texts, WhatsApp, web chat and Instagram messages, and AI even replies to your Google reviews — like a front desk that never sleeps.

Is there a contract?

No. The CRM is billed month-to-month with no long-term contract and no minimum term. It's built to pay for itself in recovered bookings — if it doesn't, you cancel.
