About the Studio

Hand-Coded Websites, Built by a Developer

Apex Web Studio is a Mississauga-based web design studio that builds fast, custom, hand-coded websites for small businesses across Canada and the USA. No page builders, no bloated templates — just clean code engineered to load instantly and win customers.

Our StoryWhy We Build Differently

Most small-business websites are built on page builders and heavy themes that look fine on day one and then load slowly, break on updates, and quietly lose customers. Apex Web Studio was founded to fix that. Every site we ship is hand-coded from scratch — semantic, secure, and engineered for speed.

That approach is measurable, not marketing: across our live pages, server response times average under 100 milliseconds — a fraction of the multi-second loads common on template builders. Fast sites rank better on Google, convert more visitors, and are far easier for AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity to read and recommend.

We work with small businesses the way a technical partner should: flexible monthly plans with no large upfront cost, all-inclusive hosting and maintenance, and a real person who answers when something needs to change.
